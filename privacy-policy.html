Privacy Policy

Riftbound Score Tracker · Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Riftbound Score Tracker (the “App”) handles your information. Your privacy is important to us. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive device data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App saves certain preferences and game data locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Game setup (player names, colors, chosen legends, number of players, scoring options).
  • App configuration (selected language and how the Utilities panel is opened).
  • Calculator states (Battlefield Might, Ornn Calculator, and Runes tracker values).
  • Whether the in-app tutorial has been shown.

This information is stored using your device's local storage. It never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or any third party, and is not accessible to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, etc.).
  • No location data.
  • No usage analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or other third-party SDKs that collect personal data. The App works entirely offline and does not send your data over the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Data Security

Because all data remains on your device and is not transmitted, your information is protected by your device's own security measures.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
